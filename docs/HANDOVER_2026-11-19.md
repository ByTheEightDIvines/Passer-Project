## What 6c513f4 changes (plain terms, for the user)

- **Wagons follow the ground.** The hitch and rear are probed for ground height, and the harness and coach pitch up and down slopes. They no longer float. (`wagon_actor.cpp` placeParts)
- **Drivers stay seated.** Their walking-legs layer is off while they sit (bench pose = anim 14), so they no longer walk in place. Hits on drivers or bench sitters play the sitting flinch.
  - Drivers get down and run only when:
    - the wagon is wrecked (existing behaviour), or
    - every escort is dead (new: `c.abandoned`). The wagon then stands, and the convoy ends once it is out of sight.
- **No walking off edges.**
  - Fighters' `stepToward` checks the ground ahead; if there's a gap or a drop of more than 120, they turn but don't step.
  - Held escorts do the same check in `placeHeld`.
  - Where the wagon's side isn't safe (a bridge edge, a drop or a wall), escorts walk single file behind the wagon. This uses `sideSafe` / `escortSpot`, rechecked every 15 frames.
- **Enemies on ledges.** `reachableFrom` samples the ground every 60 units and checks for climbs, gaps and big drops; it is cached for 10 frames per fighter.
  - Out of reach:
    - spear and sword fighters don't charge; they face the enemy with the shield raised (`fighter_anim::raiseShield`, the "block" anim);
    - archers shoot it;
    - camp guards hold too.
  - Convoy, foe unreachable from the wagon and no live archer escort: `c.fleeing` is set. The wagon drives at 2x speed, escorts stay in formation and run, and nobody fights.
- **Weapons stowed in the march.** When an escort goes back into formation, `fighter_anim::drop` puts the shield and spear on his back.
- **Back shields flipped** to crest out, grip against the back. The user saw the render `back_flipped.png` and did not object.
  - The four `kHold_back_{tall,small}_{man_a,mad_a}` matrices in `guard_gear.inc` were rotated 180° about the shield's vertical axis and re-offset so the inner face stays where it was. The spear is still underneath.
  - In-hand shields are **not** changed. The user decided they were right all along: "the feet being there is just throwing me off".
- **Bard music diagnostics only, no fix.** `camp_music.cpp` now logs, at +15, +90 and +300 frames and every 900 frames while playing:
  - whether each of the three sound handles is still alive;
  - the volumes;
  - Link's distance;
  - the sound IDs.

  Next: ask the user to stand by a bard for about 15 seconds, then read `travellers_diag.log` (path below) and look for `music … alive`.
  - If the handles die right away, the sound or wave setup is the problem: `mod_sounds::addSound` / `loadWave`. Note there are 112 sounds, exactly kMaxSounds.
  - If the handles are alive at volume and still silent, the cause is the category, the 3D position or the mixer.

  Don't guess.
